# Verification

An independent verifier tried to refute the claims that most decide the Surat vs Ahmedabad comparison.
Its verdicts:
- **confirmed:** the claim holds.
- **refuted:** the claim is wrong.
- **corrected:** the claim is partly right.
- **unverifiable:** the verifier could not check it.
