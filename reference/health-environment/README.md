## What each document supports

- **amc-air-quality-data-2018-2020.pdf**: AMC ambient air quality annual averages by station — health-environment _Licence: Municipal (AMC) publication; reproduced for reference_
- **amc-health-wellness-page-2026-09-28.html**: AMC Health & Wellness page loads but only links a malaria info file (404) and old TB documents, so there are no case counts (health-environment dimension) _Licence: Amdavad Municipal Corporation publication; reproduced for reference_
- **gujarat-gpcb-air-quality-monitoring-2024-25.pdf**: GPCB runs NAMP at 43 and SAMP at 26 stations incl. Ahmedabad and Surat, with 2024-25 annual averages in annexures; image-only PDF: health-environment dimension _Licence: Government of Gujarat (GPCB) publication; reproduced for reference_
- **gujarat-gpcb-ncap-page-2026-09-27.html**: GPCB states Ahmedabad and Surat are NCAP first-list non-attainment cities, with a three-tier committee and city action plans on PRANA: health-environment dimension _Licence: Government of Gujarat (GPCB) publication; reproduced for reference_
- **gujarat-gpcb-water-quality-monitoring-2024-25.pdf**: GPCB publishes statewide water quality monitoring (rivers incl. Tapi/Sabarmati, lakes like Kankaria, groundwater), not ward-wise city results, so RTI is needed: health-environment dimension _Licence: Government of Gujarat (GPCB) publication; reproduced for reference_
- **india-cpcb-aqi-bulletin-2026-09-24.pdf**: CPCB station counts: Ahmedabad 9 CAAQMS stations, Surat 2, closing the unverified station-count gap (health-environment dimension) _Licence: Government of India (CPCB) publication; reproduced for reference_
- **india-cpcb-aqi-bulletin-2026-09-25.pdf**: CPCB daily AQI with city station counts (Ahmedabad 112 Moderate, 8/9 stations) (health-environment dimension) _Licence: Government of India (CPCB) publication; reproduced for reference_
- **india-cpcb-aqi-bulletin-2026-09-26.pdf**: CPCB daily AQI with city station counts (Ahmedabad 93 Satisfactory, 8/9 stations) (health-environment dimension) _Licence: Government of India (CPCB) publication; reproduced for reference_
- **india-cpcb-caaqms-station-feed-2026-09-27.json**: CPCB station feed works without a key and gives per-city station lists for Ahmedabad and Surat (health-environment dimension) _Licence: Government of India (CPCB) public feed; reproduced for reference_
- **india-mohua-pib-eoli-mpi-2020-results-2021-03-04.html**: EoLI 2020 million+: Ahmedabad 3, Surat 5; MPI 2020: Surat 2 - health/environment dimension _Licence: Government of India press release; reproduced for reference_
- **india-mohua-swachh-survekshan-million-plus-2024-25.csv**: SS2024-25: Ahmedabad #1 cleanest in 10 lakh+ category (Surat in Super Swachh League) — health/environment dimension _Licence: MoHUA Swachh Survekshan results; OpenCity dataset licence 'Other (Public Domain)'_
- **india-mohua-swachh-survekshan-report-2024-25.pdf**: SS2024-25 results: Ahmedabad #1 (10 lakh+), Surat #2 in Super Swachh League — health/environment dimension _Licence: Government of India (MoHUA) publication, mirrored on OpenCity portal (licence 'Other (Public Domain)'); reproduced for reference_
- **india-mohua-swachh-survekshan-results-2022.csv**: Swachh Survekshan ranking history: Surat #2 in SS2022 — health/environment dimension _Licence: MoHUA Swachh Survekshan results; OpenCity dataset licence 'Other (Public Domain)'_
- **nfhs5-mirror-district-indicators-2019-21.csv**: NFHS-5 district CSV gives ~104 indicators each for Surat and Ahmedabad districts (district, not city scope) - health dimension _Licence: CC-BY 4.0 (Harvard Dataverse DOI 10.7910/DVN/42WNZF); underlying data from IIPS/MoHFW NFHS-5 district factsheets_
- **smc-air-quality-results-index-2026-09-27.html**: Surat publishes no current air-quality readings on its portal; only station PDFs up to 2021 and a blank real-time widget — health/environment dimension _Licence: Surat Municipal Corporation (public authority) web page; reproduced for reference_
- **smc-aq-varachha-monthly-reports-2021.pdf**: SMC's sensor-based CAQMS (Varachha) results published as monthly PDF reports - health-environment air quality row _Licence: Surat Municipal Corporation / Surat Smart City SPV publication; reproduced for reference_
- **smc-caaqms-2026-09-28.html**: Surat air-quality network: 2 SMC sensor CAQMS, 7 GPCB manual stations, 7 NCAP CAAQMS planned - health-environment _Licence: Surat Municipal Corporation publication; reproduced for reference_
- **smc-disease-incidence-home-2026-09-27.html**: SMC disease incidence is only via a date/disease query tool covering 8 hospitals; no bulk disease stats on portal — health dimension _Licence: Surat Municipal Corporation web page; reproduced for reference_
- **smc-disease-incidence-statistics-2026-09-28.html**: Refutes 'no disease surveillance on portal': SMC has 7 HTML tables of monthly cases and deaths: health-environment dimension _Licence: Surat Municipal Corporation web page; reproduced for reference_
- **smc-drainage-performance-1995-2012.html**: Surat sanitation/water statistics on the portal are historical only (stale, to 2011-12) — health/environment dimension _Licence: Surat Municipal Corporation (public authority) web page; reproduced for reference_
- **smc-smimer-hospital-statistics-2026-09-27.html**: SMC publishes year-wise hospital service statistics for its medical college hospital (SMIMER), usable in a health module _Licence: Surat Municipal Corporation web page; reproduced for reference_
- **smc-swm-statistics-2026-09-28.html**: Refutes 'no public SWM tonnage': 8 monthly tables, e.g. July 2026 door-to-door 678 vehicles, 2,440.77 MT/day: health-environment dimension _Licence: Surat Municipal Corporation web page; reproduced for reference_
- **smc-vbdc-activity-statistics-2026-09-27.html**: SMC publishes vector-control activity data but not ward-wise case counts - health-environment / RTI request for dengue/malaria cases _Licence: Surat Municipal Corporation publication; reproduced for reference_
- **smc-vbdc-annual-report-2018.pdf**: Only VBD statistics on SMC portal are in a dated (~2018) annual report; no current data — health dimension _Licence: Surat Municipal Corporation publication; reproduced for reference_
- **smc-vbdc-home-2026-09-28.html**: SMC VBDC page is descriptive (489 surveillance workers, 39 UHCs), no case counts - health-environment vector-borne row _Licence: Surat Municipal Corporation publication; reproduced for reference_
- **smc-water-quality-monitoring-2026-09-27.html**: SMC publishes city-level water sampling regime (IS:10500, ~220 lab samples/day) but no ward-wise test results - health-environment / RTI gap _Licence: Surat Municipal Corporation publication; reproduced for reference_
- **smc-water-supply-hydraulic-home-2026-09-28.html**: Surat water supply data is only a historical narrative (e.g. 440 MLD / 150 lpcd by 2001) — health/environment dimension _Licence: Surat Municipal Corporation (public authority) web page; reproduced for reference_
- **smc-water-supply-present-status-2015.html**: Latest SMC water-supply figures on the portal date from 2015 (stale) — health/environment dimension _Licence: Surat Municipal Corporation (public authority) web page; reproduced for reference_
